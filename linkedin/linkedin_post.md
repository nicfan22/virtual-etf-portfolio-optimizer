# LinkedIn Post Draft

I built a Virtual ETF Portfolio Optimization & Risk Management Engine in Python.

The goal was to simulate how an asset manager, robo-advisor, or quantitative investment team could research ETF allocation strategies before deploying capital.

The system analyzes a diversified ETF universe across equities, bonds, real estate, commodities, gold, and international markets. It includes:

- ETF data collection and validation
- Return, volatility, covariance, and correlation analysis
- Equal Weight, Minimum Volatility, Maximum Sharpe, Risk Parity, and Maximum Diversification portfolios
- Walk-forward out-of-sample backtesting
- Simulated transaction costs
- Benchmark comparison versus SPY and a 60/40 portfolio
- Institutional risk metrics: CAGR, volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, max drawdown, VaR, CVaR, alpha, beta, and turnover
- Interactive Plotly charts
- Editable Streamlit dashboard
- GitHub Pages static report for a public URL

This project helped me strengthen my understanding of portfolio construction, risk management, optimization, backtesting, and financial application engineering.
